How to Play

🐤 Flappy Hop

Controls: Space, ↑, click or tap to flap.
Goal: Fly through the gaps in the pillars. Each gap = 1 point. Touching a pillar or the ground ends the run.

🐍 Snake

Controls: Arrow keys or WASD; swipe on a touch screen.
Goal: Eat the red food to grow (+10). Hitting a wall or yourself ends the game. You get faster as you grow.

🏏 Slam Over

Controls: Space, click or tap to swing.
Goal: You face 12 balls with 3 wickets. Swing when the ball is over the dashed ring: dead centre = 6, then 4, 2 and 1 runs. Swinging too early/late, or letting the ball pass, costs a wicket.

♟️ Chess

Controls: Click a piece, then click a highlighted square. White moves first.
Goal: Checkmate the other king. Only legal moves are allowed, and a king in check is highlighted red. (Castling and en passant are not included; pawns promote to queens.)

⏯ All games

Use Start, Pause (or P) and Restart under each game. After a game, save your score with a name to appear on the leaderboard.
